# Message and channel system

## Message hierarchy

Build public messaging in this order:

1. **Recognition:** a clear current identity and domain.
2. **Relevance:** the problems or systems that matter to the target audience.
3. **Difference:** a defensible combination of experience, approach, or perspective.
4. **Proof:** selected work that makes the difference believable.
5. **Direction:** where the professional is deliberately heading.
6. **Action:** what the visitor can inspect or do next.

One element may carry more than one job, but do not compress the whole strategy into a slogan.

## Proof-backed pillars

For every message pillar, record:

| Pillar | Audience relevance | Evidence | Accurate claim | Channel |
|---|---|---|---|---|

If a pillar has no evidence, classify it as direction or remove it. If evidence is private, use the narrowest public statement that remains defensible.

## Professional story

Use a selective through-line:

- starting context;
- one or two meaningful changes or decisions;
- the repeated problem or interest connecting the work;
- the present focus;
- the next direction.

The story should explain the position. It should not reproduce the complete employment chronology or force a dramatic origin narrative.

## Channel roles

### Website

Owns the overall interpretation: position, selected work, case-study context, limits, and contact route.

### GitHub

Owns inspectable technical depth: code, documentation, tests, issues, releases, and reproduction information. Repository activity alone is not proof of competence.

### LinkedIn

Owns professional discovery and a current, concise public context. Use familiar vocabulary without keyword stuffing or unsupported seniority.

### CV

Owns concise chronology tailored to an opportunity. It may select evidence differently while preserving the same facts.

### Interview

Owns interactive explanation: why decisions were made, what failed, what was learned, and what the person would change.

## Calls to action

Prefer a small set:

- inspect a selected case;
- inspect the corresponding repository;
- view or download a current CV;
- visit the canonical LinkedIn profile;
- start a relevant professional conversation.

Do not add social channels, newsletters, booking funnels, downloads, or forms merely to make the presence look complete.
