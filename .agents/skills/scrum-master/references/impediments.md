# Record an impediment

An impediment is a concrete condition stopping a selected product task. Record:

- the affected task and product;
- what is missing, with evidence or source;
- what work it prevents;
- the next action or decision needed;
- whether the user needs to act.

Keep the note in that product's current sprint or backlog. Do not maintain one cross-product impediment register. If one external dependency affects both websites, link a short note from each relevant product record rather than merging their Scrums. Report when the blocker changes; do not invent age, escalation, or an owner.
