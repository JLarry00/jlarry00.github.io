# Homepage review gate

## Content and positioning

- Can a new visitor state the person's current identity, demonstrated focus, and direction after the opening screen?
- Is the main professional objective visible without an inflated title?
- Does each prominent claim have a route to proof?
- Are project maturity, ownership, and limitations accurate?
- Is the next useful action clear?
- Does the page avoid reading as a full CV or a generic landing page?

## Information architecture

- Does section order follow the intended visitor path?
- Does every section answer an audience question or enable an action?
- Are the strongest proof units easiest to find?
- Do links set accurate expectations about repositories, cases, CVs, and external profiles?
- Are language variants complete and factually aligned?

## Visual system

- Does the direction emerge from the person's work rather than a reusable tech aesthetic?
- Are typography, spacing, color, and imagery coherent at actual viewport sizes?
- Is hierarchy understandable without animation?
- Are diagrams, code, badges, logos, and decorative controls doing necessary work?
- Are project differences reflected in their presentation?

## Responsive and interaction

- Inspect representative wide desktop, laptop, narrow mobile, and larger mobile viewports.
- Check overflow, zoom, long titles, translated text, navigation, cards, media, and touch targets.
- Complete the primary path with keyboard only.
- Verify visible focus, logical focus order, skip behavior where needed, and no hover-only information.
- Check reduced-motion behavior and that essential content works without client JavaScript.

## Accessibility and readability

- Verify semantic headings, landmarks, link purpose, form labels, alternative text, and language attributes.
- Measure relevant foreground/background contrast rather than judging it by eye.
- Check line length, font size, spacing, and reading order.
- Ensure status and meaning do not rely on color or motion alone.

## Performance and resilience

- Record the build result and console errors.
- Inspect image, font, script, and animation cost.
- Test broken or slow assets and external-link behavior where practical.
- Record measured performance with environment and method; do not report unmeasured scores.

## Evidence report

For every finding state:

- viewport or environment;
- exact path and interaction;
- observed result;
- expected result;
- severity and audience impact;
- whether automated, manually verified, visually inspected, or still untested.
