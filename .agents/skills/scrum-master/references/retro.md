# Lightweight retrospective

Use this when a delivery cycle exposed useful process friction; do not schedule a ritual just to satisfy a template.

1. Review the previous improvement action, if one exists. Mark it done, still open, or dropped with the reason.
2. Use concrete evidence from the cycle: rework, missed acceptance checks, unclear scope, useful safeguards, or a blocker. Separate observation from interpretation.
3. Choose at most a small, actionable change with an observable check. Do not invent an owner or due date; Juan may own a decision.
4. Record the note in the affected product's closed sprint file or retrospective section. Carry an open action into that product's next sprint or backlog.

Do not calculate team health or trends from a single cycle. In a solo workspace, process improvements should address actual friction in the collaboration rather than imitate a team's ceremony.
