ABOUT

A technical path shaped by curiosity.

I am drawn to work that turns a messy process into something clearer, more useful and easier to improve.

From technical production to software

My path began with technical production work at 2EyesVision. It taught me to care about details, handoffs and the quality of a process before I started studying Computer Engineering at Universidad Autónoma de Madrid.

Automation, infrastructure and applied AI

During my time at Thinkia, I explored DevOps and applied AI in a practical setting. That experience reinforced an interest in automation that is understandable to the people who have to use and maintain it.

Where I am heading

I am now orienting my learning toward agentic AI: systems that can help with real work while keeping their decisions, checks and boundaries visible. My degree is ongoing, and my final-degree project is still in planning, with its scope open.

Beyond the screen

I also follow economics and investing. Studying Japanese and travelling to Japan have been ways to keep learning outside computing, with the same interest in how people, systems and contexts fit together.
