# Audiences and decision journey

## Core audience lenses

### Recruiter or sourcer

- Decision: whether the profile plausibly matches an open or future role.
- Needs: clear category, location or eligibility when relevant, credible keywords in context, concise chronology, and working links.
- Typical limit: brief initial scan.
- Useful next step: CV, LinkedIn, or a short selected-work overview.

### Hiring decision-maker

- Decision: whether this person may solve relevant problems and deserves deeper evaluation.
- Needs: scope, ownership, trajectory, judgment, collaboration, and evidence of delivery or learning.
- Useful next step: a focused case study or conversation.

### Technical evaluator

- Decision: whether the person can reason, build, verify, and explain at the required level.
- Needs: architecture, tradeoffs, code or artifacts, tests, failure handling, limits, and accurate maturity labels.
- Useful next step: repository, technical case, demonstration, or interview.

### Peer or collaborator

- Decision: whether the person's work and perspective merit attention or collaboration.
- Needs: a concrete shared problem, reusable artifact, clear point of view, and an appropriate contact route.

Add other audiences only when they affect a real objective.

## Decision journey

### Discover

The person appears in search, a referral, GitHub, LinkedIn, an application, or a shared artifact. The signal must be recognizable without inflated titles.

### Understand

The audience can state who the person is, what systems or problems they focus on, and what direction they are pursuing.

### Believe

Claims connect to inspectable projects, experience, decisions, outcomes, or learning. Maturity and ownership are explicit.

### Investigate

The audience can choose the depth it needs: case study, source code, documentation, CV, or external evidence. Links and labels must set accurate expectations.

### Contact

The next action is clear, safe, and proportional. A job seeker may invite relevant professional conversations without manufacturing scarcity or sales pressure.

## Objection mapping

For each audience, record:

| Objection or uncertainty | Honest answer | Supporting proof | Remaining gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| What level is this work? | Exact maturity label | Repository, test, deployment, or document | What has not been tested |
| What did this person own? | Specific contribution | Commit, artifact, decision, or account | Team or AI contribution |
| Is the stated direction credible? | Current evidence plus explicit direction | Repeated relevant work | Next proof to build |

Do not hide a gap. Decide whether to qualify the message, build evidence, or remove the claim.
