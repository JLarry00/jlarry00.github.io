# Process checks for this workspace

- **Mixed product plans:** a change to one website appears only in the other website's Scrum. Move or cross-reference it explicitly without copying a shared backlog.
- **Silent scope drift:** new work arrives during a sprint without a dated reason or effect on the goal.
- **Done by intention:** an item is marked complete because it was planned, rather than because its acceptance check has evidence.
- **Synthetic Scrum:** fixed durations, daily updates, points, velocity, burndown, or team-health scores are added without a real need and source.
- **History rewritten:** a closed sprint is edited to conceal what was incomplete. Preserve it and append a dated correction instead.
- **Private context published:** imported CVs, private notes, credentials, local paths, or workspace instructions are copied into a public website or deploy artifact.
